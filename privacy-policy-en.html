Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 18, 2026

Thank you for using the "Diet Assistant" app (the "App"). Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what data the App processes and how it is protected.

1. Data We Collect

The App does not send any data to a server and does not collect any data. The following information is stored only on your device (local storage):

InformationPurposeWhere stored
Profile data (name, age, height, weight, gender, goals)Diet plan and body analysis calculationsOn device only (local storage)
Lab test valuesParameter interpretation and suggestionsOn device only
Tracking logs (weight, water, steps, sleep)Progress chartsOn device only

2. Data Sharing

Your health and diet data (profile, lab results, tracking logs) is never shared, sold, or transferred to any third party and remains on your device only. In the free (ad-supported) version, only the advertising-related technical identifiers described in 3. Ads may be processed with Google LLC.

3. Ads and Third-Party SDKs (Google AdMob)

The free version displays banner ads served by Google AdMob at the bottom of the app. These ads may process technical data such as the advertising ID, device model, language, and coarse location (IP-based country level) with Google LLC for ad delivery and fraud prevention. The ad SDK cannot access your health data.

  • Users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and Türkiye are shown Google's consent form (UMP) for personalized ads; you can change your choice at any time via the in-app Privacy Options button.
  • Non-personal / advertising-identifier data is processed under Google's privacy policies.
  • The app offers a 30-day free trial from first installation; during the trial and while an Ad-Free Premium subscription is active, ads are removed entirely and the ad SDK transmits no data.
  • Subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed solely by Google Play / the App Store; no card or payment details are shared with the developer. Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period and can be cancelled anytime from your store account.

4. Permissions and File Access

  • File selection: A file is accessed only when you choose it for lab report import.
  • Saving files: Diet plan and backup files are created only when you request them.
  • Printing: Used only when you request printing.

5. Data Security

All data stays on your device. Deleting the App or using the "Reset" function permanently removes this data. Because the App works offline, no data is transmitted over the network.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Health Information Disclaimer

The App is for general informational purposes only and is not a substitute for medical advice. Please consult your physician about health issues.

8. Changes

When this policy is updated, it will be published in the App and on this page.
